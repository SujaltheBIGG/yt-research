The verdict

Stories beat tips.

Personal finance and investing belong to big channels with a face on camera. Business stories, money history and economics explainers are where new faceless channels break through.

What the winners have in common

No face

Every winner is faceless. A clear voice, strong visuals and a real story carry the video.

Thumbnail

One strong image and 2 to 4 big words with emotion.

GAME OVER · QUIETLY GOING BUST · WHAT WENT WRONG?

Title

A few formulas repeat across channels.

X Explained Like You're 5 · The Rise (And Fall?) of X in 11 Minutes · WTF Is Happening to X · Why X Failed

Topic and length

Famous companies and money events, from Rockefeller to the AI bubble. Mostly 11 to 25 minutes.
